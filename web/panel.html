Welcome to CopyOver
Your clipboard on all your devices, over your own network. Three steps and you’re done.
  1. Install the shortcuts or the app. On iPhone and iPad, the iOS shortcuts; on Android, the APK from the latest release.
  2. Link your phone. Tap Link phone at the top and scan the QR code with the camera.
  3. Copy and paste. Copy on one device and paste on the other: text, photos and files.
